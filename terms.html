Responsible use

Users must have the rights and permissions required to save media. Respect copyright, creator choices, platform terms, provider terms, and applicable laws.

Service limitations

ClipNest accepts public direct video-file URLs and public post links supported by its configured SaveAPI provider. Availability depends on the platform and specific post. Resolved video files are limited to 250 MiB. Direct video-file URLs retain their original bytes; supported social videos are converted to H.264/AAC MP4 (up to 720p) for broader device playback. The temporary converted file is removed after download or when its token expires, up to 10 minutes. Users must confirm the rights holder permits the download and the platform allows it. ClipNest does not request social-account passwords or cookies, access private content, or bypass DRM or access controls. SaveAPI may charge credits for lookups; users and operators are responsible for reviewing current provider pricing and terms.
