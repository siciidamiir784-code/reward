GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Can I paste a YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, or Facebook post?

ClipNest uses SaveAPI to request a real video file for supported links and validates the returned file before preview and download. YouTube uses SaveAPI’s YouTube download endpoint, which costs 10 credits and requires your plan to support it. If no actual video file is returned or the file fails validation, ClipNest shows an error; thumbnails, watch pages, and embedded streams are never downloads. Other unsupported, private, or unavailable posts also show an error. Use YouTube Studio for your own uploads.

Why might a download fail?

The source must be publicly reachable and return a video file. Private, sign-in-only, expired, blocked, unsupported, or non-video links are not supported. The server accepts standard HTTP and HTTPS ports only.

Is there a download size limit?

Yes. Video files are limited to 250 MiB. Direct files and provider-returned MP4s remain original; other social formats are converted to H.264/AAC MP4 up to 720p for broader device playback.

Does ClipNest store my URL or file?

ClipNest temporarily keeps a random download token and source URL in server memory for up to 10 minutes. When a social post is submitted, its URL is also sent to SaveAPI to resolve the media. Direct and provider-returned MP4 bytes are streamed without being saved. Other social source video is streamed through FFmpeg; its converted output is stored temporarily and removed after download or within 10 minutes. Review the privacy policy and SaveAPI terms before launch.
