Links and media

When a user submits a direct video-file URL, ClipNest's server requests that URL and streams the response to the browser without storing the video on disk. When a user submits a recognized social post URL, ClipNest sends that URL to SaveAPI using a server-side credential to resolve an eligible media URL. The source video is streamed through FFmpeg and converted to H.264/AAC MP4; only the converted output is temporarily stored in a unique server temp directory until the download completes or its token expires (up to 10 minutes). A server or machine crash may leave temporary output for the host to clean up. SaveAPI processes the submitted URL under its own terms and privacy policy; it receives the URL and ClipNest server's network address. The video source and ClipNest server may also receive the user's IP address. Configure server and host logging, retention, access, and third-party processing to match your actual deployment and disclose it here.

Advertising and analytics

ClipNest displays Native Banner and 300×250 banner ads supplied by Adsterra. Their ad code loads third-party scripts from bellnewyork.org and aqml.org, and ad images or tracking resources from static.nresystems.com and cdn.storageimagedisplay.com. When the scripts run, Adsterra and its advertising partners may receive data such as a visitor's IP address, browser/device information, and page/request details, and may use cookies or similar technologies according to their settings and policies. ClipNest does not control the ads or the data those third parties collect. Review Adsterra's current privacy and cookie information and applicable consent requirements. This notice is general information, not legal advice or a substitute for any consent controls required for your visitors.
